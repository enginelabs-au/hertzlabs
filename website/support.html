Frequently asked

Do I need headphones?

For binaural modes, yes. Any stereo headphones or earphones work, and over-ear headphones with good channel separation work best. Speakers play mono, so use Acoustic Resonance mode if you want a speaker-friendly session.

Can I play my own music at the same time?

Yes. Start Spotify, Apple Music or any other audio app, then return to Hertz Labs. The two mix automatically.

Why does audio stop when my screen locks?

On the free plan, audio stops when the app is minimised or the screen locks. Premium users can turn on background playback in the Background tab.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the paywall from any “Upgrade to Premium” button and tap restore at the bottom. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you purchased with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel through your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. For Apple, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and find your Hertz Labs purchase. For Google Play, request a refund from your Play order history.

What does the AI Guide do?

Describe a goal or how you want to feel, and it suggests frequency settings or a session. Suggestions are for personal wellness only, not medical advice.

How do promos and offer codes work?

Complete optional activities in the Promos hub to earn App Store or Google Play codes, redeemed through the store. Some rewards need manual approval.

How do I send feedback?

Open the footer menu in the app and choose Feedback. Affiliate applications need your email address.

Is it safe to use?

Hertz Labs is designed for relaxation and focus. Use a comfortable volume and take breaks. Don’t use it if you have epilepsy, photosensitivity or seizure susceptibility, and check with a doctor if you’re pregnant, have a heart condition or are unsure.
