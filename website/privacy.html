Privacy

No account needed. Your sessions and settings stay on your device.

Last updated July 2026 · Applies to Hertz Labs on iOS, Android and Mac

01Overview

Hertz Labs is a binaural beats and audio frequency app operated by Engine Labs. There is no account to create: no username, no password. Most activity is tied only to an anonymous subscriber ID on your device.

03Data that stays on your device

  • Motion sensor data (gyroscope and accelerometer), used to shape audio in real time in Kinetic Mode
  • Session and frequency settings, kept in encrypted local storage
  • App preferences and state

This data is never transmitted.

04Third-party services

  • RevenueCat manages in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google Gemini API powers the AI Guide and processes your text prompts.
  • Supabase hosts backend functions for feedback, promo rewards, referrals and app update policy.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play process payments under their own privacy policies.

Each service handles data under its own privacy policy.

05Affiliate applications

Affiliate applications sent through the in-app Feedback form need an email address, your message and attached device metadata. We use this to assess your application and, if approved, to manage the partnership. Tax or payout details may be requested separately by email.

06Children

Hertz Labs is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

07Retention

RevenueCat identifiers follow RevenueCat's retention policy. AI prompts sent to Google follow Google's data handling policies. Feedback and affiliate emails are kept only as long as needed to respond or manage a partnership, then deleted or archived.

08Your rights

Because most data is anonymous, there is usually no profile tied to your identity. If you gave us your email through Feedback or an affiliate application, you can ask to access or delete that correspondence by emailing support@enginelabs.com.au or hello@enginelabs.com.au. Requests about RevenueCat or Google data go to those providers.

09Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes are reflected in the date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
